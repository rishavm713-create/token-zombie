# SUTRA INTELLIGENCE

**The intelligence control architecture behind everything by Rishav Mishra.**

SUTRA INTELLIGENCE is an independent, model-agnostic architecture for making AI systems more deliberate, dependable and efficient. It works *around* AI models, not instead of them. It helps a system understand the goal, keep the right context, choose the right tools, check its work and say honestly what happened.

*Sutra* means "thread": many parts, held together by one thread.

## The story

> **Open parts. One mind.**
> The tools are built in the open where that helps people. The thinking that makes them work together is SUTRA INTELLIGENCE, and it stays ours.

- **TOKEN ZOMBIE** is the open body: a free plugin that stops Claude Code wasting your usage.
- **SUTRA INTELLIGENCE** is the mind: the design, the fine-tuning and the way the parts work together.
- Every product below is built on it.
