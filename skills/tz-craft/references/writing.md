# Docs and interface text

- Put the answer or action first; background after, or not at all.
- Write to the reader as "you", in present tense and active voice.
- One idea per sentence, one topic per paragraph. Cut words that change nothing ("simply", "just", "in order to").
- Use one term for one thing across the product and docs.
- Name buttons by what they do ("Delete project"), not "OK" or "Submit".
- An error message says what happened, why if known, and what to do next. No blame, no bare codes.
- Commands and examples must be copyable and tested; mark placeholders clearly.
- Link text describes the destination.
- State numbers with units and how they were obtained; drop claims you cannot show.
- When reviewing, report file:line, the problem and the replacement text.
